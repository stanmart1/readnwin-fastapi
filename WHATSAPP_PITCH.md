## Tips for Sending:

1. **Choose the version** that matches your relationship with the recipient
2. **Personalize** with their actual bookstore name and any specific details you know
3. **Send during business hours** (10 AM - 5 PM works best)
4. **Follow up** after 2-3 days if no response
5. **Be ready** with your demo link and calendar availability
6. **Keep it conversational** - WhatsApp is informal, so don't be too stiff

## Recommended Approach:

**Day 1:** Send Version 1 or 8 (friendly, not too long)
**Day 3:** If no response, send Version 6 (ultra-short reminder)
**Day 7:** If still no response, send Version 7 (benefit-heavy, different angle)

Good luck! 🚀
